# Manual check

About ten minutes. The automated tests check that the controls work. These are the things they cannot judge. Open the built page, set a key you know, add Cm7, F7, and Bbmaj7, choose Drop-2, and press Play.

- Playing the progression feels in time. The click of each step lands where you expect, and nothing rushes or drags.
- The motion of each voice is easy to follow on the neck. You can watch one note move to the next without losing it among the others.
- The staff can be read at a glance. Clefs, spacing, and the current measure are obvious without leaning in.
- The colors tell the root, 3rd, 5th, and 7th apart. You can name a tone's role from its color alone.
- Nothing on the page is still cluttered or confusing. You can find the next thing you meant to do.
- It sounds right on your speakers. The chords are the ones named on the steps, at a volume you can sit with.
- A real MIDI keyboard lights the right notes. The key you play shows up on the neck and the keyboard as that pitch, not the one beside it.

If one of these fails, note which step you were on and what you saw or heard.
